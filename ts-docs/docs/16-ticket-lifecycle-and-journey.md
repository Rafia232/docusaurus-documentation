# Ticket Lifecycle Process

The following flow explains the complete journey of a support ticket, starting from ticket creation by a Facility User and ending with resolution, closure, and reporting.

---

## Complete Ticket Lifecycle Flow

**1. Facility User Logs In**  
The Facility User opens the configured **Public Portal URL** and logs in using the email address and password created by the administrator.  
↓  

**2. Facility User Opens a New Ticket**  
The Facility User selects **Open a New Ticket** from the portal navigation.  
↓  

**3. Facility User Enters Ticket Information**  
The user provides:

- Ticket Title
- Detailed Description
- Category
- Subcategory
- Application
- Priority Level
- Supporting Attachments, when required

The user’s contact information, assigned facility, and region are linked to the ticket.  
↓  

**4. Facility User Submits the Ticket**  
The Facility User clicks **Save** to submit the ticket.  
↓  

**5. Ticket Is Created with Open Status**  
The system generates a unique Ticket Number and records:

- Created Date
- Region
- Location or Facility
- Category and Subcategory
- Application
- Priority
- SLA Due Date
- Facility User information

The ticket initially enters the **Open** status.  
↓  

**6. Automated Notifications Are Sent**  
Based on the configured Automation Rules, email notifications may be sent to:

- Organization Administrator
- Admin
- Supervisor
- Assigned Person
- Facility User

↓  

**7. Ticket Appears as Unassigned**  
If no user was selected during ticket creation, the ticket appears as **Unassigned**.

For Regional Users, it becomes available under:

- **All** – Shows unassigned tickets in the assigned region and tickets assigned to the logged-in user.
- **Unassigned** – Shows only tickets in the assigned region that do not have an assignee.

National Users and Administrators can view the ticket from the organization-wide ticket list.  
↓  

**8. Regional, National, or Administrative User Reviews the Ticket**  
An authorized support user opens the ticket and reviews:

- Ticket Title and Description
- Region and Location
- Category and Subcategory
- Application
- Priority
- Due Date
- Facility User information
- Attachments

↓  

**9. Ticket Is Assigned**  
The ticket is assigned from the **Assign** tab.

A Regional User may assign the ticket to:

- Themselves
- Another Regional User within the same region
- A National User
- An Administrator

A National User or Administrator may assign or reassign the ticket to another eligible support user across the organization.  
↓  

**10. Assigned User Becomes the Ticket Owner**  
The most recently assigned user becomes the **Ticket Owner**.

The assignment is recorded in the **Activity** tab, and the ticket appears in the assigned Regional User’s **My Tickets** list when applicable.  
↓  

**11. Ticket Moves to In Progress**  
When the Ticket Owner starts working on the issue, the ticket status changes from **Open** to **In Progress**.  
↓  

**12. Ticket Owner Investigates the Issue**  
The Ticket Owner:

- Reviews the ticket information
- Opens the attached files
- Checks the ticket history
- Investigates the reported issue
- Coordinates with other technicians when required

↓  

**13. Internal Communication Is Added**  
The Ticket Owner can post an **Internal Note** for communication between technicians and support staff.

Internal Notes may contain:

- Technical findings
- Troubleshooting information
- Handover instructions
- Internal escalation details

Internal Notes are not visible to the Facility User.  
↓  

**14. Support User Sends a Staff Post**  
The Ticket Owner can post a normal reply without selecting any special option.

This creates a **Staff Post**, which is visible to the Facility User.

Staff Posts may be used to:

- Request additional information
- Provide troubleshooting steps
- Share progress updates
- Ask the Facility User to test a solution

↓  

**15. Facility User Receives and Reviews the Reply**  
The Facility User opens the ticket from the Public Portal and reviews the technician’s reply in the ticket conversation.  
↓  

**16. Facility User Sends a Client Post**  
The Facility User can reply with:

- Additional information
- Answers to technician questions
- Screenshots
- Documents
- Confirmation of test results
- Further explanation of the issue

The Facility User’s reply appears as a **Client Post** in the Threads tab.  
↓  

**17. Ticket Investigation Continues**  
The Ticket Owner reviews the Client Post and continues troubleshooting.

During this stage, the ticket may be:

- Updated
- Reassigned
- Escalated to a National User
- Escalated to an Administrator
- Supported by another technician

All significant actions are recorded in the **Activity** tab.  
↓  

**18. SLA Is Monitored**  
The ticket is monitored against the SLA time configured for its priority.

If the ticket passes its due date without completion, it becomes **Overdue**.

An Overdue Ticket Alert may be sent according to the configured Automation Rules.  
↓  

**19. Issue Is Resolved**  
After completing the required troubleshooting or corrective action, the Ticket Owner confirms that the issue has been resolved.  
↓  

**20. Final Response Is Posted**  
The Ticket Owner can close the ticket using either of the following options:

### Post as Resolution

The response:

- Is visible to the Facility User
- Is recorded as the final resolution
- Closes the ticket

### Close Ticket on Reply

The response:

- Is posted as a normal Staff Post
- Is visible to the Facility User
- Closes the ticket

↓  

**21. Ticket Status Changes to Closed**  
The ticket status changes to **Closed**, and the closure is recorded in the Activity Timeline.  
↓  

**22. Facility User Reviews the Resolution**  
The Facility User can open the ticket from the Public Portal and review:

- Final resolution
- Technician responses
- Conversation history
- Attachments
- Closed status

The Facility User may also close the ticket after confirming that the issue has been resolved, where this option is available.  
↓  

**23. Ticket Data Updates Dashboards and Reports**  
The completed ticket contributes to:

- Technician Dashboard
- Regional Dashboard
- Management Dashboard
- Executive Dashboard
- SLA reports
- Technician performance reports
- Facility and regional reports
- Recurring category reports
- Resolution and response-time reports

## Simplified Ticket Lifecycle

**Facility User Logs In**  
↓  
**Creates and Submits Ticket**  
↓  
**Ticket Enters Open Status**  
↓  
**Notifications Are Sent**  
↓  
**Ticket Appears as Unassigned**  
↓  
**Regional, National, or Administrative User Reviews Ticket**  
↓  
**Ticket Is Assigned**  
↓  
**Assigned User Becomes Ticket Owner**  
↓  
**Ticket Moves to In Progress**  
↓  
**Technician Investigates the Issue**  
↓  
**Internal Notes and Staff Posts Are Added**  
↓  
**Facility User Responds with Client Posts**  
↓  
**Ticket Is Updated, Reassigned, or Escalated When Required**  
↓  
**SLA and Due Date Are Monitored**  
↓  
**Issue Is Resolved**  
↓  
**Resolution or Closing Reply Is Posted**  
↓  
**Ticket Status Changes to Closed**  
↓  
**Ticket Data Is Included in Dashboards and Reports**
